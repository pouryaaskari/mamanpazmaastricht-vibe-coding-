Our roots

The Story Behind mamanpaz Maastricht

Our family comes from Iran, a country where food is deeply connected to tradition, hospitality, and togetherness. For us, sharing a meal has always been about more than just eating. It is about bringing people closer, creating memories, and making everyone feel welcome.

Made with love

Our Mother's Passion for Cooking

At the heart of mamanpaz is our mother's passion for cooking. Her love for preparing food and creating warm, authentic flavors inspired us to bring our family's cooking to Maastricht.

The recipes we serve are inspired by the tastes and traditions of our Iranian heritage. They reflect the warmth and authenticity that make our food special to us. Through mamanpaz, we want to share that feeling with our guests and introduce them to flavors that mean so much to our family.

From our family to yours

From Our Family to Yours

mamanpaz was born from our mother's passion for cooking and our family's love of food. We wanted to create a place where people could enjoy delicious food in a welcoming atmosphere, surrounded by friends, family, and good company.

Whether you visit us for a casual meal, a family gathering, or a special celebration, we hope you feel the same warmth and hospitality that have always been important to us.

For us, mamanpaz is more than a restaurant. It is a way to share our family's love for food, our Iranian roots, and the joy of bringing people together.
